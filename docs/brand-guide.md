# Project RESET digital brand guide

This is the implementation reference for Project RESET digital products. It translates the approved `RESET Design Guide` and the later `Project RESET Creative Samples` into repeatable interface rules. If this document conflicts with newly approved artwork or direction from the Foundation, the newer approved source wins and this file should be updated.

## Brand idea

Project RESET moves people from awareness to agency. Every experience should make a meaningful next step feel smaller, clearer, and freely chosen.

- Platform line: `Choose Better. Together.`
- `Choose Better` means asking for one attainable choice rather than everything at once.
- `Together` means recovery and systems change are collective, not solitary.
- The tone is warm, direct, thoughtful, and non-judgmental. Avoid clinical distance, forced optimism, or language that turns reflection into a test.

## Source hierarchy

1. Approved Project RESET wordmarks, collages, and exported artwork
2. `RESET Design Guide.pdf`, version 1.0, August 2026
3. Approved directions in `Project RESET Creative Samples.pdf`, September 2026
4. This implementation guide
5. Existing product patterns, when they do not conflict with the above

Do not recreate or recolor an approved export when the correct variant is available. Request a new variant when needed.

## Core color system

HEX values are authoritative.

| Token | HEX | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sindoor | `#DE5240` | Primary campaign color and primary action |
| Kalika | `#1D1D1D` | Dark background and primary text |
| Shweta | `#FFFFFF` | Light background and reversed text |
| Marigold Light | `#FBCC64` | Reserved for `re` and the period on a Sindoor wordmark; not a general accent |
| Pavitra | `#EDBAA6` | Supporting pale accent and selected-state surface |
| Mrida | `#52292B` | Supporting dark burgundy |

Use no more than two background colors on one visual surface. Sindoor remains the campaign color on master collateral.

### Accessibility

- White on Kalika passes for text at all supported sizes.
- Kalika on Marigold Light and Kalika on Restore Sky pass at all supported sizes.
- White on Sindoor is reserved for large text and headlines. Use Kalika for smaller copy on Sindoor.
- Never communicate meaning with color alone. Write the associated label.

## The five pathways

| Order | Pathway | HEX | Supporting phrase |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Nourish | `#458284` | Plants, water, earth |
| 2 | Restore | `#82BCC8` | Sleep, stillness |
| 3 | Move | `#DE5240` | Body in motion |
| 4 | Connect | `#FA8757` | People, animals |
| 5 | Rebalance | `#D4953B` | Limits, meaning |

The complete pathway strip is always five equal segments in this order. It can be used as a footer rule, card spine, cover edge, or shared-community marker.

Pathway colors identify actual pathways. Do not assign them decoratively to unrelated topics, questions, or interface states. Do not mix two pathway colors on a surface. When an item represents everyone, use the full five-band strip rather than choosing one pathway color.

## Wordmark and lockup

- The branded mark is lowercase `reset.`
- On a light ground, use the approved two-tone mark.
- On Sindoor, `re` and the period use Marigold Light.
- On Kalika, `re` and the period use Connect Sevantika.
- Use a one-color mark only for small or single-color applications.
- Never stack, letterspace, distort, or freely recolor the wordmark.
- Maintain clear space equal to the height of the `r` on all sides.
- Minimum width is 72 px on screen.
- Use the full `PROJECT / reset. / Choose Better. Together.` lockup at first encounters when space allows.
- When the constructed mark cannot be rendered, write `RESET`. Use lowercase `reset` only when it is grammatically a verb.

The documentary title is always bold Poppins capitals and larger than its qualifier:

`THIRD DEGREE BURNOUT: A SURVIVOR'S GUIDE`

## Typography

| Role | Typeface and treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Headlines and wordmark | Poppins SemiBold |
| Body | Poppins Regular |
| Documentary title | Poppins Bold, uppercase |
| Labels and credentials | Poppins SemiBold, uppercase, approximately `0.24em` tracking |
| Pull quotes | EB Garamond |
| Platform line | Approved custom script; Petit Formal Script is the current digital stand-in |

Script is an accent, not a body typeface. In normal interfaces, use it only for the platform line or a very brief approved emotional accent.

The Learning Lab word clouds are an intentional exception. Their varied EB Garamond weights, italics, sizes, colors, and motion are part of the visualization and should not be standardized into the interface font.

## Composition

The visual language is editorial rather than app-like.

- Prefer square corners, ruled grids, strong alignment, generous negative space, and bold type hierarchy.
- Alternate disciplined Kalika, Shweta, and Sindoor sections instead of introducing near-white or generic gray card backgrounds.
- Use solid colors. Do not introduce gradients or decorative textures.
- Avoid generic floating cards, soft drop shadows, glass effects, rounded dashboard tiles, and decorative color-coding.
- Use borders and background reversals to create hierarchy.
- Keep a clear focal point. Supporting elements should not compete with the primary question or action.

### Torn band and crimp edge

The full documentary band is a torn Sindoor or pathway-color block at the foot of a composition. Its edge is irregular, it carries the RESET wordmark, and it appears only once on a surface. It does not float or rotate.

The event creative also establishes a smaller `crimp` edge for separating white, Kalika, and Sindoor fields. Prefer the sharper, more frequent crimp. A crimp is a separator, not a second full band, and should remain subordinate to the content.

Do not combine the full torn band and another competing torn shape on the same surface. The five-band pathway strip may coexist as a thin footer or spine when the layout needs an all-pathways marker.

## Collage and photography

- Human faces and figures are black and white.
- Surrounding paper, clip art, objects, plants, animals, and newsprint remain fully saturated.
- Use deckled or torn collage edges, not clean vector cutouts.
- Use a white or single-color ground with no gradient or texture.
- Do not add drop shadows to collage elements.
- Keep a collage whole when the approved layout calls for it. Do not crop indiscriminately.
- Use one approved art set per surface. Do not combine Pramana, Samudra, and Jana.

Approved art-set roles:

- Pramana: film, evidence, launch, and first-encounter moments
- Samudra: food systems, climate, and environmental stories
- Jana: participation, pledge, and community moments

## Partners and attribution

JIVINITI and Picture Motion appear at equal visual height. On Sindoor or Kalika, place partner marks together on a white plate with adequate padding and rounded corners so the JIVINITI leaf remains visible. Other partner logos are visually separated and accompanied by their role.

Partner logos never touch the RESET wordmark and never sit inside the full torn band.

## Digital interaction translation

Digital motion and state changes should support comprehension.

- A control that changes color must also produce a clear labelled change nearby.
- On theme selection, move focus to the updated heading and scroll gently to it.
- Reveal substantial content over roughly 600-800 ms with a restrained stagger. Do not snap or flash.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing decorative movement.
- Use Sindoor for the primary action. Secondary actions are outlined in the text color of their surface.
- Selected states require text or icon confirmation in addition to color.
- Keep questions browseable. Do not impose artificial progress, timers, or completion gates.
- Keep safety, privacy, and optionality language plain and visible without making it visually dominant.

### Take It to the Table

The conversation tool should feel like the hero and topic selector belong to the same editorial object.

- Hero: Kalika identity header, Shweta content field, approved collage, and one Sindoor crimp transition.
- Theme selector: Shweta surface, square ruled grid, and explicit selected state.
- Question library: Shweta surface, Sindoor crimp transition, black ruled grid, coral numbering, and a single Sindoor action hierarchy. It should feel continuous with the theme selector rather than introducing a new dark mode.
- The question themes are film themes, not RESET pathways. Do not assign pathway colors to question cards.
- The five-band strip remains at the shared footer as the all-pathways marker.

## Review checklist

Before approving a new digital surface, confirm:

- The official RESET mark has the correct color variant, spacing, and minimum size.
- The documentary is named correctly where attribution is required.
- Poppins carries interface copy; script and EB Garamond remain limited to approved roles.
- Every color is from the palette and serves its documented role.
- No more than two background colors compete on a single surface.
- Pathway colors correspond to pathways or appear together in the ordered five-band strip.
- Collage treatment follows the grayscale-person, saturated-surround rule.
- There are no gradients, collage shadows, or generic floating app cards.
- The primary action is obvious and accessible without relying on color alone.
- Motion is gentle, purposeful, and removed for reduced-motion users.
- Mobile layouts remain legible with no clipped text or horizontal overflow.
- JIVINITI and Picture Motion attribution is present and legible where required.
